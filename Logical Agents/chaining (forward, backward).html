Forward and Backward Chaining

Follow data-driven and goal-driven Horn-clause inference one step at a time.

Horn-rule knowledge base

Facts: A (sensor active), B (battery charged).

Rules: R₁: A ∧ B ⇒ C (robot ready); R₂: C ⇒ D (may begin inspection); R₃: D ⇒ E (inspection available); R₄: B ⇒ F (battery sufficient).

Query: Is E derivable?

Choose a method

Why the procedures differ

Forward: Data-driven

Start with A and B. Fire every newly applicable rule. It may derive F even when the question asks only about E.

Backward: Goal-driven

Start from E, then seek rules that establish E and recursively check their prerequisites. Avoids the unrelated F fact.
