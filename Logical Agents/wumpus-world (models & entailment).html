Wumpus World: Models & Entailment

Filter hypothetical worlds using percepts and test logical conclusions.

Knowledge base: two observations

Rules: B₁₁ ↔ (P₁₂ ∨ P₂₁); B₂₁ ↔ (P₁₁ ∨ P₂₂ ∨ P₃₁). The start is known pit-free, and there is no breeze at [1,1]. Hence P₁₂ = false. In this three-variable model, B₂₁ ↔ (P₂₂ ∨ P₃₁).

In this demo, “safe” means pit-free, not necessarily free of the Wumpus.
