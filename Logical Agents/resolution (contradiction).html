Resolution: Proof by Contradiction

Follow a CNF resolution refutation for a Wumpus World safety claim.

Resolution proof: Is ¬P₁₂ entailed?

Knowledge base: B₁₁ ↔ (P₁₂ ∨ P₂₁) and observation ¬B₁₁. We want to prove ¬P₁₂, meaning [1,2] has no pit.

Initial clauses: C₁: (¬B₁₁ ∨ P₁₂ ∨ P₂₁), C₂: (¬P₁₂ ∨ B₁₁), C₃: (¬P₂₁ ∨ B₁₁), C₄: ¬B₁₁.

Negated query: C₅: P₁₂.

Why contradiction proves entailment

KB ⊨ α ⇔ KB ∧ ¬α is unsatisfiable

To prove α = ¬P₁₂, temporarily assume P₁₂. If the clauses resolve to the empty clause □, the assumption contradicts the KB.
